How it works

  • Each mark you place sends your opponent to the matching box. Mark the top-center cell of any box, and they must play in the top-center box.
  • If that box is closed (captured or full), they can play in any open box.
  • Capturing a box sets the opponent free: after any capture (including echo chains), their next move can be in any open box.
  • Capturing a box also marks the matching cell in every other open box (echo marks). Echoes are real marks: they can complete lines and chain-capture more boxes.
  • A captured box becomes that player's mark on the big board. 3 captured boxes in a row wins — nothing else. If every box closes with no row of 3, it's a draw (box count is just for info).
