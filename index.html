How it works

1 · You send them

Your mark sends your opponent to the matching box. Play the top-center cell of any box, and they must play in the top-center box. If that box is closed, they can play in any open box.

2 · Captures echo

Capturing a box marks the matching cell in every other open box. Echoes are real marks: they can complete lines and chain-capture more boxes. After any capture, the opponent can play in any open box.

3 · Three boxes win

A captured box counts as your mark on the big board. Three captured boxes in a row wins. If every box closes with no row of three, it's a draw.
